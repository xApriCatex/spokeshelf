Spokeshelf

An audiobook player for iPhone, for the books you already own as files: M4B with its chapters, MP3, M4A, AAC, FLAC, WAV and AIFF. No adverts, no account, no subscription, and no internet connection needed.

Help and contact

Questions, problems or ideas: dimroom.app@outlook.com. Please say which iPhone and which version of Spokeshelf you have (Settings in the app).

Common questions

How do I get books in? On the iPhone: save the files to the Files app (iCloud Drive, On My iPhone or a USB drive), then in Spokeshelf tap Import (the + at the top of the Library tab, or Import from Files in Settings) and pick a book's files. You can also open an audio file in Spokeshelf from another app's share sheet. From a Mac or PC: connect the iPhone, open Finder (Mac) or the Apple Devices app (Windows), choose Files, and drag whole book folders onto Spokeshelf.

How does Spokeshelf decide what is one book? A folder is one book, with CD 1, Disc 2 and similar sub-folders joined in order. A single M4B file is one book. When you pick loose files, files that share an album tag become one book.

Does Spokeshelf stream, sell or download books? No. It plays files you already have: DRM-free purchases, ripped CDs, public-domain recordings such as LibriVox, and your own recordings.

Can it play Audible books? Not copy-protected ones. Audible's AAX and AA files only play in Audible's own app, and protected M4P files from the iTunes Store only play in Apple's apps, so Spokeshelf cannot play them and says so when you try to import one.

Where are my books? In Spokeshelf's own folder, which you can open in the Files app (On My iPhone, then Spokeshelf). They are ordinary files, not locked inside the app.

A book shows the wrong title or no cover. Spokeshelf reads the title, author and cover art saved inside the files. If those are missing, it uses the folder or file name. Fix the tags with a tag editor on your computer and copy the book in again.

Where are the chapters? An M4B file usually carries its own chapters, and Spokeshelf shows them. A book made of many MP3 files gets one chapter per file.

Does it keep my place? Yes. Every book keeps its own place, saved while it plays, when you pause and on every jump, so you can switch books and pick up where you left off.

What is Spokeshelf Pro? A one-off purchase, not a subscription. It adds a speed remembered for each book, skip buttons set to your own lengths (5 to 90 seconds), three more colour themes (Night, Moss and Ember) and saving a library backup. Everything else stays free, and restoring a backup is free.

Pro on a new phone or after reinstalling: sign in with the same Apple account, open Spokeshelf Pro in Settings and tap Restore purchase. You do not pay again.

Moving to a new phone: copy your books across as you first put them in. To keep your place in every book and every bookmark, save a library backup in Settings on the old phone (Pro) and restore it in Settings on the new one (free). The backup holds your places, bookmarks and book names, not the audio files.

Deleting the app deletes your books. Your audiobooks live in Spokeshelf's folder, so keep your own copies on a computer or in iCloud Drive before you delete the app.
