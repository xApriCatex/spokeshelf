Privacy policy

For the Spokeshelf iPhone app. Last updated 23 November 2026.

The short version

Spokeshelf collects no data. There is no account, no sign-in, no adverts and no analytics, and it needs no internet connection. We never see your books or your library.

Who we are

Spokeshelf is made by Milad Sharhani, an independent developer in the United Kingdom. Contact: dimroom.app@outlook.com.

Where your books are kept

Your audiobooks are files in Spokeshelf's own folder on your iPhone, which you can see in the Files app. Your place in each book, your bookmarks and your settings are saved in Spokeshelf's own storage on your iPhone. Cover art read from your books is kept as small picture files there too, and removed when the book is gone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. If your phone makes its own backup (for example iCloud Backup), that backup includes them, under your Apple account's privacy terms.

When anything leaves your phone

Spokeshelf itself sends your library nowhere. It leaves the phone in two ways only: in your phone's own backups (such as iCloud Backup), if you have them turned on, under your Apple account's terms; and in a library backup you choose to save, which goes wherever you pick in the share sheet. What happens to it there is up to you and that app. The backup is first written to a temporary file for the share sheet; Spokeshelf deletes it when sharing ends, or at the next start if the app was closed first.

A library backup holds no audio, but it does hold the names of your books (titles, albums, file and folder names, lengths and sizes), your place in each one and when you last listened, which books you finished, and your bookmarks with their notes and dates. Treat it like any private note.

Buying Spokeshelf Pro

Spokeshelf Pro is a one-off purchase made through Apple. Apple handles the payment and we never see your card or Apple account details; the app only learns whether your Apple account owns Pro, and remembers that in the iPhone's keychain so Pro works offline. To do that, the app asks Apple's store whether your account owns Pro, and for its price, when it starts and when you come back to it. Nothing about your books or listening goes with that check, and listening works with no connection at all.

Permissions

Spokeshelf asks for no permissions. Importing books and restoring a backup use the iPhone's own file picker, which shows the app only the files you choose. The lock screen and Control Center show the book and chapter that are playing.

Deleting your data

Deleting a book from its menu in Spokeshelf deletes its files and bookmarks. Deleting the app removes your books, places, bookmarks and settings from your iPhone, except a small note in the iPhone's keychain saying whether it has Pro, which can stay until the phone is reset. Files you have saved elsewhere and your phone's own backups are not touched; delete those where they are. Because nothing is stored with us, there is nothing else to delete.

Children

Spokeshelf collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes
